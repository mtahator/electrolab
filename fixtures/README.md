## Paper mix (P0.C.1)

## Register

The register is [`papers.toml`](papers.toml): every paper with its exact versioned URL, SHA-256, licence, source and what it covers. `cargo xtask fetch-fixtures` downloads the papers into `.fixtures/papers/` (about 100 MB, not in Git) and verifies every hash. A source that regenerates its file (Wikipedia PDF export) is committed in `papers/` instead (`committed = true`).

Current set (P0.C.1, 2026-11-12): 17 arXiv papers (CC BY 4.0, licence confirmed through arXiv OAI-PMH), NACA Report 1135 (public domain, scanned with an OCR text layer), one PLOS ONE paper (CC BY 4.0), one Persian Wikipedia article (CC BY-SA 4.0, right-to-left). Six two-column, six with no PDF outline, two over 200 pages (212 and 286), one with mixed page sizes.
